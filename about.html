About SkyNest Co-Living Spaces

A modern home designed for your flexible lifestyle

SkyNest Co-Living Spaces is a modern co-living company based in Singapore that provides flexible, short-term and long-term accommodation for young professionals, digital nomads, and international students[cite: 1].

Who we are

With a big rise in demand driven by remote work and an increase in people relocating for work and study[cite: 1], finding a seamless, well-designed living experience has never been more important.

SkyNest combines fully furnished private rooms, high-speed Wi-Fi, and inspiring shared co-working spaces to create an environment where you can live productively and comfortably[cite: 2].

Our diverse package options offer different levels of privacy and flexibility, helping you choose an arrangement that fits your unique schedule.

Our purpose

To deliver modern, engaging accommodation and clear guidance that bridges the gap between high-quality services and effortless digital booking[cite: 2].

We aim to support our residents with transparent information on our packages, comprehensive facilities, and accessible support for custom and corporate requirements[cite: 2].

A space to connect

Shared social areas and community events give residents somewhere to relax, collaborate, and build meaningful connections.

Our accommodation packages

Choose from our carefully structured stays designed to match your duration and comfort level.

Premium Stay

Private studio apartment with comprehensive luxury inclusions.

  • Weekly cleaning service
  • Utilities & high-speed internet included
  • Access to all building facilities
  • Monthly community events pass

Standard Stay

Private bedroom in a shared apartment for a balanced co-living experience.

  • Fortnightly cleaning
  • Utilities included
  • Access to co-working space
  • Shared kitchen and lounge access

Short-Term & Custom

Flexible stays or tailored arrangements for special requirements.

  • Flexible booking (1–4 weeks) for short stays
  • Corporate bookings and long-term options
  • Accessibility requirements accommodated
  • Contact company to arrange custom plans

All packages are subject to availability. Custom packages require direct consultation with our team to arrange specific accessibility needs[cite: 2].
