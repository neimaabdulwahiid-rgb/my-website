Discover SkyNest

Everything you need under one roof

Designed for modern remote work and hassle-free living, our spaces combine private comfort with vibrant shared amenities.

Move-in ready comfort

Enjoy fully furnished private rooms with utilities, laundry rooms, and 24/7 key-card security included so you can settle in right away.

Work and study productively

Stay connected with building-wide high-speed Wi-Fi, shared co-working spaces, and quiet study pods tailored for remote professionals and students.

Wellness and community

Unwind in our rooftop garden, gym and yoga studio, or communal kitchens, and connect with fellow residents at weekly community events.

Flexible packages to suit your stay

Whether you are visiting Singapore for a few weeks or settling in for the long term, choose a package that fits your schedule:

  • Premium Stay: Private studio apartment with weekly cleaning, utilities, high-speed internet, access to all facilities, and a monthly community events pass.
  • Standard Stay: Private bedroom in a shared apartment with fortnightly cleaning, utilities, co-working access, and a shared kitchen and lounge.
  • Short-Term Stay: Flexible 1–4 week bookings in a furnished room with utilities included and limited access to shared facilities.
  • Custom Packages: Tailored options for corporate bookings, long-term stays, or residents with specific accessibility needs.
