Facilities

Designed for remote workers, digital nomads, and international students in Singapore, our building pairs high-speed connectivity and productive workspaces with vibrant social and wellness amenities.

Shared spaces & amenities

Social lounge, kitchens & rooftop

Unwind after work or connect with fellow residents during our weekly community events across our shared lifestyle spaces:

  • Social lounge and weekly community events
  • Communal kitchens and dining areas
  • Rooftop garden, gym, and yoga studio

Co-working spaces & study pods

Stay focused with dedicated work and study zones equipped for remote professionals and students:

  • Shared co-working spaces for collaboration
  • Quiet study pods for focused individual work
  • High-speed Wi-Fi throughout the building

Fully furnished private rooms & essentials

Every resident enjoys a fully furnished private room or studio designed for immediate move-in comfort, backed by utilities included across all packages.

For everyday convenience and peace of mind, the building also provides on-site laundry rooms as well as 24/7 security and key-card access. Note that access levels to shared facilities vary depending on your selected stay package.
